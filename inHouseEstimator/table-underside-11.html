1.0 poker table ›

Heat sensors only: no chairs, tags, batteries or receivers. Table 4 from the Floor page (its game and seats). Live: a ghost sits in every seat with a signed-in player (green) or a Guest (purple). Tap any part to see what it is.

3DDrag to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag (two fingers) to slide.

What's on it

Heat sensor (MLX90614), one per seat under the rail, tilted out and down. The orange cone is what it sees: a lap or a standing player's waist.
ESP32 hub under the middle of the table. Reads all 6 sensors and sends them to the tablet over Bluetooth.
Seat cables, one per seat back to the hub: 12 V for the charger, plus 5 V, ground, data and clock for the sensor.
USB-C fast charger in the rail at each seat, next to the NFC sticker.
NFC sticker in the rail at each seat. Tap a phone to sign in to that seat.
Dealer's tablet runs Dealer Control. The dashed blue line is the Bluetooth link from the hub.
12 V power supply under the table (chargers run on 12 V; a step-down makes 5 V for the hub and sensors), cord down the pedestal to the floor outlet.

How it works

  • The player signs in at the seat (NFC sticker or seat QR). From then on the heat sensor keeps their hours: warm = playing, cold = on break.
  • The sensor tilts out from under the rail, so it sees a seated lap and a player standing at the seat.
  • Someone warm at a seat (sitting or standing) for 3 seconds or more who hasn't signed in pops up as a Guest. Guest sessions under 10 minutes are dropped; the rest stay in the table's log so the floor can give the hours to the right player.
  • Seat changes: the dealer drags the player's name to the new seat in Dealer Control.
  • Tablet off = table closed, so nothing is lost when the tablet is off.
