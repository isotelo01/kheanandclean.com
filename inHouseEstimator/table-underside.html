Under the Table

What's on the drawing

R1–R4 receivers (UWB anchors, Qorvo DWM3001CDK, about 2×1 in). Spread out toward the four corners, not bunched together, so they can place every chair. Hidden under the table; the radio goes through wood and plastic, not metal.
Raspberry Pi (main box): the Pi and its power supply in one box, between the pedestals. Takes seats 1–5 and receivers R2, R3, and sends everything over Wi-Fi to the app's database.
Side box: its own power supply, seats 6–9 (and 10 for tournaments) and receivers R1, R4.
Seat module at each seat, under the rail: heat sensor (aimed down at the lap) and USB-C phone charger in one case.
Two pedestals with round bases. Cables and boxes stay clear of them.
Seat cables: one Cat6 per seat, straight to its box. It carries the 12 V for the charger and the heat sensor's data.
Receiver cables: each receiver's micro USB cable plugs into a USB jack on the nearest box.
Link cables: two Cat6 between the boxes, one for sensor data (yellow), one for the side box's receivers through a USB extender (green).
Tablet power: 12 V from the main box to the dealer's tablet.
Power cords: both boxes and the shuffler plug into the power strip; its cord runs down the right pedestal to the floor outlet.

Good to know

  • Chairs aren't on this drawing. Each chair carries its own tag and swappable 18650 battery under the seat; nothing wires to the table.
  • Heat sensors on one cable each need their own address. The cheap MLX90614 can be given one in software; the grid AMG8833 only has two, so it needs a small splitter board (I2C multiplexer).
  • Charging ports: each box's supply is sized for all its seats charging at once (about 24 W each).
  • Setup once per table: measure where each receiver sits, mark each seat, and take an empty-seat reading for the heat sensors. Everything is mounted on the table, so the table can move without redoing it. Only redo it if a receiver gets moved.
  • Wall power: one cord from the floor outlet runs up the right pedestal to a power strip under the table. Both boxes' power supplies and the shuffle machine (on its own cord) plug into the strip.
  • The dealer's tablet gets 12 V from the main box; the shuffle machine plugs into the power strip. The shuffler sits on the dealer's right, in front of the chip tray (in front of the tablet); its top is flush with the felt and its body hangs under the table (see the 3D view).
  • Keep metal away from the receivers and the chair tags.
