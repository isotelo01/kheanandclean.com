Flat drawing

What's on the drawing

R1–R4 receivers (UWB anchors, Qorvo DWM3001CDK, about 2×1 in). Spread out toward the four corners, not bunched together, so they can place every chair. Hidden under the table; the radio goes through wood and plastic, not metal.
Hub (Raspberry Pi 5) under the middle. Collects where the chairs are and who's sitting, and sends it over Wi-Fi to the app's database. The tablet, the Floor page and the wait list all read it from there.
Heat sensor at each seat, under the rail, aimed down at the lap. Needs an open hole to look through.
USB-C charging port at each seat, under the rail, for the players' phones.
Two pedestals (the table's feet), one toward each end, like a real poker table. Keep cables and boxes clear of them.
Power supply: one plug to the wall runs everything on the table.
Receiver cables: one USB cable from each receiver to the hub (data and power).
Heat sensor cable: one thin 4-wire cable runs seat to seat under the rail and back to the hub.
Charging power: a power run under the rail out to every USB-C port. Wiring details later.

Good to know

  • Chairs aren't on this drawing. Each chair carries its own tag and swappable 18650 battery under the seat; nothing wires to the table.
  • Heat sensors on one cable each need their own address. The cheap MLX90614 can be given one in software; the grid AMG8833 only has two, so it needs a small splitter board (I2C multiplexer).
  • Charging ports: plan the power supply for every seat charging at once (USB-C phones pull about 20 W each).
  • Setup once per table: measure where each receiver sits, mark each seat, and take an empty-seat reading for the heat sensors. Everything is mounted on the table, so the table can move without redoing it. Only redo it if a receiver gets moved.
  • The dealer's tablet and the shuffle machine run off the same power supply. The shuffler sits on the dealer's right, in front of the chip tray (in front of the tablet); its top is flush with the felt and its body hangs under the table (see the 3D view).
  • Wire management: one cable track clipped around the underside just inside the rail carries every cable (heat sensors, receivers, charging power). Each seat gets a short drop from it to its heat sensor and USB-C port; the hub, power supply, tablet and shuffler each tie into it. Everything mounts flat against the underside, clear of players' knees.
  • Newer plan (Hardware page): one Cat6 cable per seat runs straight back to a hub box that holds the hub and the 12 V power supply; the shuffler plugs into the power strip on its own cord. The drawing and 3D view still show the older cable track.
  • Keep metal away from the receivers and the chair tags.
