Before you shoot

  1. Lay a sheet of printer paper flat on the floor near the middle of the room. It's the ruler.
  2. Turn on the lights and open the blinds.
  3. Hold the phone sideways at chest height and step back into the corner as far as you can.
  4. Get the floor edges in every shot, where the walls meet the floor, plus the paper.
  5. Shoot closets and nooks in the Extra boxes if they're getting cleaned.
