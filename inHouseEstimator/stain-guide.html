Stain Guide

Tap a stain or type it. Every product here is from your SDS binder. Always test a hidden spot first and never mix products.

Coffee, tea, cola

Tannin stains. Also some berry, ink and dye spots.

Prochem Coffee & Tannin Spotter Ready to use · pH 3.5 · synthetic fibers
  1. Extract or blot up as much of the spill as you can.
  2. Apply straight from the bottle onto the stain.
  3. Work it in gently, then blot with a white towel.

The product page doesn't give a dwell time.

Rinse: plain water, then extract. It rinses easily with water.

Still there? Stain 1, or Stain Magic for old or tough ones.

Pet urine (odor + stain)

Also feces odor and wet-dog smell.

ODORx Un-Duz-It Unleashed pH 9 · OK on wool
  1. Pour it on so it reaches everything the urine reached, pad included. It only works where it touches.
  2. Use it straight, or dilute up to 1:1 with water.
  3. Let it sit 1 hour.
  4. Deep extract.
Rinse: extract with cold or warm tap water.

Yellow left behind? Stain 1 (synthetics only), or Yellow Rx for browning.

Heads up: odor in the pad or subfloor may not come out. Don't promise it.

Poop (on nylon or polyester)

Your 4-step method from Mark.

  1. Scrape and remove all solids. Blot.
  2. Acid step: Coffee & Tannin Spotter. Apply, work in, blot.
  3. Alkaline step: Ultrapac Extreme. Apply, work in, extract.
  4. Enzyme: Un-Duz-It Unleashed for the odor. Let it sit 1 hour, then extract.
  5. Brown-out: Yellow Rx if a brown ring is left.
Rinse: extract with water between steps. Finish with All Fiber Rinse after the alkaline step.

Heads up: Ultrapac Extreme is pH 11.7. Some carpet makers don't allow cleaners above pH 10.

Blood, vomit, fruit juice, plant stains

Organic and natural stains. Also coffee, tea and urine stains.

Pros' Choice Stain 1 One-part · use straight · pH 6.5 · oxidizer
  1. Test a hidden spot for color.
  2. Clean the area first to get out as much as you can, then blot the fibers dry.
  3. Spray it on heavy to soak the stained fibers. A trigger sprayer works best.
  4. Let it work. It can clear right away or take a few hours.
  5. Optional: cover with clear plastic to help it work.
  6. Stain coming back? Spray, agitate, blot, lightly spray again and leave it.

The product page doesn't give a rinse step.

Not on wool, sisal or natural fibers. OK on stain-resistant nylon.

Wine, grape juice, mustard, curry, furniture stains

Tough or old organic stains.

Pros' Choice Stain Magic (Part A + Part B) Mixed pH 9 · ammonia smell
  1. Test a hidden spot. Some dye loss is always possible.
  2. Extract the spot, then blot as dry as you can.
  3. Mix equal parts A and B in a clean, open container or the dual sprayer. Use it within 5 minutes and toss leftovers.
  4. Soak the stained fibers evenly and agitate gently.
  5. Cover with clear plastic (best for mustard, curry and wood stains).
  6. Wait. It can clear right away, in 1–2 hours, or up to 12 hours.
  7. Mustard or curry: lay a damp towel over it and steam iron on the lowest setting for 1–3 minutes, checking every 15 seconds. Never put the iron on the carpet.
Rinse: hot water extraction, then dry it well.

Not on wool or natural fibers. No iron on olefin, which melts. Never close a container with mixed product in it, because it can burst. Never mix it with Red Relief.

Yellow or brown rings (browning)

From over-wetting, jute or cotton backing, or chemical residue.

Prochem Yellow Rx Acid · pH 2.5 mixed
  1. Mix 12–24 oz per gallon of water.
  2. Apply to the browned area. 1 diluted gallon covers about 400 sq ft.

The product page doesn't give dwell or rinse steps. Check the label on the bottle.

Works on nylon, olefin, cotton, jute and many natural fibers.

Rust

Pros' Choice Rust Away Use straight · pH 3
  1. Test a hidden spot. Practice on a scrap first if you can.
  2. Extraction-rinse the area first to get oils off.
  3. Apply straight onto the rust.
  4. Agitate gently with a soft brush or bone scraper.
  5. Let it sit about 1 minute.
  6. Extract, blot, and repeat if needed.
Rinse: extraction-clean the area.

Not on wool or silk. Some dye loss is possible. If nylon turns pink, apply non-sudsing ammonia and let it dry.

Gum, grease, oil, tar

Prochem Citrus Gel Gel solvent · pH 4.3 · easier on latex backing
  1. Shake it. Test a hidden spot.
  2. Scrape up as much as you can first.
  3. Apply the gel and let it sit 2–5 minutes. Don't over-apply.
  4. Scrape or blot it up with a white towel. Don't rub.
  5. On big spots, repeat and work from the outside edge in.
  6. Brush the fibers back into place.
Rinse: blot small spots with water, extract big ones with water.

Careful on carpet tiles and some latex backings. Solvents can loosen them.

Still there? Paint, Oil & Grease Remover.

Paint, lipstick, nail polish, tree sap, blacktop

Also rubber or plastic wheel marks and many inks.

Prochem Paint, Oil & Grease Remover Ready to use · solvent
  1. Test a hidden spot.
  2. Apply to the spot.

The product page doesn't give dwell or agitation steps. Check the label on the bottle.

Rinse: rinse with a water-based detergent, then extract.

Solvent-safe surfaces only. Contains 1,4-dioxane and methanol (Prop 65). Keep the area ventilated.

Ink, pen, marker

Chemspec Ink Exit pH 7.2 · no harsh solvents · won't delaminate carpet
  1. Test a hidden spot.
  2. Apply straight onto the ink.

The product page doesn't give dwell or agitation steps. Check the label on the bottle.

Rinse: rinse with your regular cleaning solution, extract, and dry it fast with an air mover.

Still there? Paint, Oil & Grease Remover handles many inks.

Graffiti, spray paint

Pros' Choice Amazing Goof Off Strong solvent

I couldn't find its directions online. Follow the label on the bottle. Test a hidden spot first, and keep the area ventilated.

No match. Try a different word, or check Stain 1 for organic stains and Citrus Gel for oily ones.

Rinsing

  • Most spotters come out with a plain water extraction.
  • After alkaline products (Ultrapac Extreme, Ultrapac Trafficlean, Power Burst, Stain Magic), rinse with All Fiber Rinse (pH 4–4.5) to bring the fibers back down.
  • Solvents (Citrus Gel, Paint, Oil & Grease Remover, Goof Off): blot or extract with water, or with a water-based detergent.
