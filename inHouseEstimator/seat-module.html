What's in it

  1. Mount (purple): a plate screwed up under the table for good. The box slides into its rails like a drawer, and one M3 screw on the side locks it into a nut in the mount, so taking the box in and out never touches the table screws.
  2. One long box, front to back: the Apple plug housing at the front, the strip in the middle, the Wi-Fi board and heat sensor at the back.
  3. Front housing: a little wider than the Apple 20 W cube, with finger holes on both sides so you can reach in and pluck the cube out. The cube plugs into the strip's end outlet, its USB-C port facing the player.
  4. Front bezel keeps the cube in: one M3 screw (about 12 mm) into a square M3 nut in a slot in the box. A hole lets the USB-C port show through.
  5. SUPERDANNY flat-plug strip (white, like the real one) in the middle, its end outlet facing front: 2 outlets on top, 1 on the end, 1 on the side, and 2 USB-A + 2 USB-C on the other side. Its flat cord comes out of the top at the back, runs down the back end, through the cord track (an L and an L) with a zip-tie slot, and out the rear to the table's power strip.
  6. XIAO ESP32C3 Wi-Fi board (blue) at the back, powered from the strip's USB port by a short right-angle cable.
  7. GY-906 BCC heat sensor (black tube, narrow view) at the back on a sled that slides out the rear. It's recessed up inside the box and angled forward, looking down through a funnel and a round IR window in the bottom toward the player's lap. If it can't see enough, we drop it lower.
  8. Round IR window: two rings snap together with black polyethylene film between them; pins on one ring poke through the film into the other.
  9. NFC sticker on the bezel. Vents on the sides.
