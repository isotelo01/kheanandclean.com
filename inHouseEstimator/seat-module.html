What's in it

  1. One long box under the rail, its front facing the player.
  2. SUPERDANNY flat-plug strip (black), turned so its top outlets face the player. Its cord comes out the right end, through the cord track (an L and an L) with a zip-tie slot, and out the back to the table's power strip.
  3. Apple 20 W USB-C cube (white), plugged into the strip's front outlet, USB-C port facing the player through the bezel.
  4. Front bezel over the cube, held by one M3 screw (about 12 mm) into a square M3 nut in a slot in the back piece. With it off, finger notches on both sides let you pinch the cube and pull it out.
  5. XIAO ESP32C3 Wi-Fi board (blue) in the right-hand bay, powered from the strip's USB port by a short right-angle cable.
  6. GY-906 heat sensor, screwed to a sled that slides in and out on rails. It sits recessed back in the right-hand bay, angled down and out, looking through a funnel that flares wider toward the front so the walls don't block its view.
  7. Round IR window at the front of the funnel: two rings snap together with the black polyethylene film between them; pins on one ring poke through the film into holes in the other so it stays tight.
  8. NFC sticker on top of the bezel (or in the rail above it). Vents on top.
