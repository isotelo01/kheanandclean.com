What's in it

  1. SUPERDANNY flat-plug strip (black): its flat plug and 3 ft cord go to the table's power strip.
  2. Apple 20 W USB-C cube (white), plugged into the strip's end outlet, port facing the player through the bezel.
  3. XIAO ESP32C3 Wi-Fi board (blue), powered from the strip's side USB port by a short right-angle cable.
  4. GY-906 heat sensor, in the front bay, tilted out and down at the player's lap.
  5. Front bezel: covers the cube, held by one M3 screw (about 12 mm) into a square M3 nut that slides into a slot in the back piece. With the bezel off, finger notches on both sides let you pinch the cube and pull it out.
  6. Back piece: holds the strip, vents on top, cord track out the back with two bends (an L and an L) and a zip-tie slot where the cord leaves.
  7. NFC sticker on top of the bezel (or in the rail above it).
