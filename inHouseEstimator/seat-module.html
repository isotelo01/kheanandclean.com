What's in it

  1. Mount (purple): a wide plate screwed up under the table for good with 9 screws (4 down each side, 1 in the middle). The box slides into its double track like a drawer until it hits the stop at the back, and one M3 screw on the side locks it into a nut in the mount, so taking the box in and out never touches the table screws.
  2. One long box, front to back: the Apple plug housing at the front, the strip in the middle, then a foil partition, then the Wi-Fi board and heat sensor at the back. It's wide enough to get a hand in and pull a plug, with room on both sides so the power cord and the USB-C cable bend in a wide curve instead of getting smashed.
  3. Thick floor: no separate tray. The strip screws straight down onto two posts on a thick floor, and a cover clips over it. The strip's front down-facing outlet is open through the bottom of the box so it can be used. The cube sits on a hollow, ribbed cradle with a foam pad.
  4. Foil partition: slides down into slots right behind the strip, foil facing the strip, with an air gap on both sides, so the strip's heat doesn't reach the sensor.
  5. Front housing: a little wider than the Apple 20 W cube, with finger holes on both sides so you can reach in and pluck the cube out. The cube plugs into the strip's end outlet, its USB-C port facing the player.
  6. Front bezel slides up into a track on the front of the box and screws in at its two top corners into square M3 nuts. A wide opening lets any USB-C cable plug in. Foam pads inside it and on the cradle squeeze the cube so it fits tight.
  7. SUPERDANNY flat-plug strip (white, like the real one) in the middle, its end outlet facing front: 2 outlets on its face (with the green and red safety lights and the two keyhole slots), facing down (the front one is open through the bottom so you can use it), 1 on each end, and 2 USB-A + 2 USB-C on one side. It's mounted outlets-down, like under a desk. Its flat cord comes out of the long side opposite the USB ports, curves back in a wide bend, runs back along that side in its own channel, past a zip-tie slot, and out the rear to the table's power strip.
  8. XIAO ESP32C3 Wi-Fi board (blue) at the back, powered from the strip's USB port by a short right-angle cable.
  9. GY-906 BCC heat sensor (black tube, narrow view) at the back on a sled that slides out the rear. It's recessed up inside the box and angled forward, looking down through a funnel and a round IR window in the bottom toward the player's lap. If it can't see enough, we drop it lower.
  10. Round IR window: two rings snap together with black polyethylene film between them; pins on one ring poke through the film into the other.
  11. Open vent slots along both sides. (The NFC sign-in sticker goes on the tabletop, not the module.)
