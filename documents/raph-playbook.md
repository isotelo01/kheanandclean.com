# Raph's Playbook (Investigator)

Lessons from Claude's hand investigations, Nov 8 2026. Raph reads this before every investigation.

## Order of work (cheapest first, stop early)
1. **Company website first.** From the site, get: office address, phone, email, names, social links, blog post dates, review badge, and any vendor page or vendor option in the contact form.
2. **Office check.** The office must be in Folsom, El Dorado Hills, Granite Bay or Roseville. Stop here if it isn't, before spending time on reviews or socials.
3. **Activity check.** Dated blog posts count. Look at the newest post. If the site shows no dates, check the Facebook page (logged in, slow, random pauses).
4. **Reviews.** Google is the main source. Web search doesn't show Google star ratings, so check Google Maps in the browser. A badge on the company's own site ("5.0 on Google") is only a lead until it's confirmed on Maps. Third-party review sites (reach150, Zillow) are extras.
5. **Way in.** Look for a vendor page, a vendor option in the contact form, a direct email, or a chamber of commerce listing.

## Skip these (dead ends found)
- **National "Folsom property management" pages** (Utopia, PURE/HomeRiver, Ziprent, Northpoint, Hemlane, Awning). These are city pages for out-of-area companies with no local office, and the top search results are full of them. Keep only a company whose own site gives a street address in the four cities.
- **Directory and aggregator pages** (Thumbtack, MapQuest, Expertise, BiggerPockets, tina.wow.ac and similar). They have little info and are often stale. Use them only to find names, then go to the real site.
- **Rental listing pages** (Zumper, Apartments.com, Rentler). These are listings, not the company.

## Careful
- **Reviewer or staff?** Review pages mix reviewers' names with agents' names. Count someone as staff only when the company site or LinkedIn says they work there.
- **Two phone numbers.** When a site shows two different phone numbers (header vs footer), record both and flag it.
- **Franchises** (Keyrenter, Real Property Management/Neighborly). The local owner makes the decisions, but corporate may control vendors. Ask whether they keep their own local vendor list.
- **Hidden emails.** Some sites hide the email behind spam protection. Use the contact form or the phone number instead. Don't guess the address.
- **A 2-year-old site edit date is a warning sign,** but check Facebook before marking the company inactive.

## Report format
TLDR → Pass/Fail on each criterion → Way in → The good / The bad → Raph's opinion → Sources.
