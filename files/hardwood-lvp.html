Hardwood & LVP Floor Cleaning in Folsom & El Dorado Hills

Luxury vinyl plank flooring provides a durable and stylish foundation for any room, but daily foot traffic, spills, and hidden grime can dull its protective finish. Standard household cleaners often leave behind a sticky residue that attracts more dirt over time. Our professional LVP cleaning service safely lifts embedded debris and restores the natural appearance of your floors without causing damage or warping. Keep your LVP looking pristine with a deep clean designed to preserve its durability and shine.

Our Professional Cleaning Process

1

Inspection & Preparation

We examine the floor condition to identify high-traffic wear zones and loose seams, clearing away surface dust and loose debris before applying any liquid cleaners.

2

Low-Moisture Pre-Treatment

Our team applies a pH-neutral, eco-friendly pre-spray specifically formulated for luxury vinyl to break down sticky residues, oil, and grime without oversaturating the planks.

3

Soft-Bristle Mechanical Agitation

We utilize specialized floor equipment equipped with ultra-soft brush agitation to gently scrub deep into the texture of the plank surface without scratching the protective wear layer.

4

Controlled Moisture Removal

Using high-efficiency controlled extraction without hot water, we immediately remove dirty water and cleaning residue, ensuring low moisture exposure to protect plank edges and adhesive layers.

5

Drying & Finishing

We quickly dry the surface and perform a light buff to eliminate streaks, restoring a uniform, beautiful luster across your entire luxury vinyl floor.

Why Regular Maintenance Matters

While LVP is renowned for its resilience, fine grit and dirt act like sandpaper over time, dulling the factory protective coating and degrading seam integrity. Regular professional maintenance prevents premature wear, eliminates stubborn build-up, and keeps your floors clean, hygienic, and vibrant for years to come.
