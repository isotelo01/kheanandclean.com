    05Method

    Five steps, in this order, every time.

    01

    Identify the fiber

    Wool, nylon, polyester and olefin each behave differently under heat and chemistry. We check before we choose a solution.

    02

    Vacuum properly

    Dry soil comes out dry. Skip this and loose dirt turns to mud the moment water reaches it.

    03

    Pre-treat and dwell

    Solution goes down and is given time to break the bond between soil and fiber before anything else happens.

    04

    Agitate

    Counter-rotating brushes lift soil to the top of the pile so extraction can actually reach it.

    05

    Extract

    Hot water extraction pulls it out. Air movers bring drying down to roughly two to four hours, depending on humidity and airflow.
