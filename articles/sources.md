## Carpet warranties

### Mohawk
- [Mohawk Care & Maintenance Guide (PDF)](https://mohawk.blob.core.windows.net/bmf/Care/care-carpet.pdf)
  - Requires professional hot water extraction every 18 months, using products, equipment or systems that carry the CRI Seal of Approval.
  - Says not to use any cleaner with a pH of 10 or higher.
  - Link checked 2026-10-29.
- [Mohawk Carpet Warranty Brochure (PDF)](https://mohawk.blob.core.windows.net/mohawkflooring/pdf/warranty/Mohawk_Carpet_Warranty.pdf)
  - Official residential warranty brochure. This copy is dated 3/2016; look for a newer one.
  - Link checked 2026-10-29.
